# Bazaar — independent creators and customers

**Real pilot entry points, currently on GitHub:**

- **Sell or receive support:** [Apply with your own Shop or public offer](https://github.com/causeway-services/.github/issues/new?template=bazaar-seller.yml).
- **Find something worth buying:** [Describe a real buyer need](https://github.com/causeway-services/.github/issues/new?template=bazaar-buyer.yml).
- **No GitHub account or need to discuss a private commercial matter?** [Contact Causeway Services](mailto:causeway@561.group?subject=Bazaar%20independent%20seller%20or%20buyer%20inquiry). Never publish confidential terms in GitHub issues.

## This is not a storefront-redirect scheme

[Shop](https://shop.561.group/) is **one accountable selling enterprise's** storefront. [Bazaar](https://bazaar.561.group/) is intended to be a place to **select and buy from multiple independently controlled sellers**. [Market](https://market.561.group/) is a broader economic observation and purchasing dashboard. The typed relationships between them do not combine merchant identities, consent, payment rights or wallets.

The existing Bazaar source currently discovers the Group's own Shop catalog and its x402 API catalog—**two catalogs are not two independent sellers**. It can inspect and compare declared items. The second original merchant's accepted live quote, safe transaction, delivered item, cancellation/refund and aftercare **have not been verified**. We will not advertise cross-seller checkout as functioning before it does.

This is an invitation to participate in a **small, permissioned pilot**, not automatic listing or an existing claim of marketplace parity.

## For a creator or shopkeeper

You can apply whether you sell digital books, downloadable artwork, physical work, a commissioned deliverable, a software API, paid membership or **voluntary donations with no promised benefit**. We will consider stores already using Stripe, PayPal or other lawful original-merchant payment providers, where a workable integration and legal terms exist.

You retain your own public identity, original shop URL, pricing, policies, payment provider, fulfillment duties, customers, cancellation/refund obligations and ability to withdraw. An opt-in issue permits us to **review your public shop**; it does not permit us to copy your customers, move funds, create a synthetic listing, insert a payment intermediary or resell anything. Our operator must separately verify that the submitter actually controls the seller or has written authority to act for them.

We will check a real public offer, original seller and payee, current availability, the relevant price/currency, rights to sell, delivery/refund policies and whether the seller consents to a specific display/checkout integration. If your current shop cannot safely support an in-Bazaar buying journey, we can discuss options without pretending a referral link is a completed Bazaar purchase. A public application has no fee; **future optional Bazaar service charges have not been set or promised at zero**.

## For a buyer

Say what you actually need, including a budget and deadline if useful. We can evaluate a comparison of eligible original sellers and report why no offer fits. **No payment is authorized by an issue.** Purchases, merchant invoices, shipping, memberships and refunds require the actual original seller's supported terms and your own separate approval.

The first meaningful pilot result is not a big number of listings. It is two independently consenting original sellers, each with one genuinely selectable, properly priced and deliverable offer, one customer completing each purchase via an authorized Bazaar buyer experience, verified original settlement and delivery, and one truthful refusal where a term or authorization is missing.

## Our actual competitive tests

| Customer job | What the existing platforms already deliver | What we would need to demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browse and buy from several independent makers | Etsy multi-seller product search, physical/digital commerce and order support | Two real consenting shops, source-correct search, in-venue separate merchant checkouts, received goods and aftercare |
| Tip a creator once or request a commission | Ko-fi one-time tips, shops and commissions | First real creator support payment and a commissioned deliverable with correct seller net proceeds |
| Join and cancel creator membership | Patreon memberships and paid content | A real tier, initial payment, renewal, member entitlement, cancellation and refund recovery |
| Fund public work repeatedly without a benefit | Liberapay recurring voluntary donations, no platform cut | One real supporter choosing and successfully funding a recurring gift, with genuine control and honest processing fees |

**Fees are not the whole job.** As of October 2026, Etsy's published US reference terms include a $0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee (and separate processing); Ko-fi Free advertises 0% platform fee for one-time tips and 5% for shops and memberships (plus processing); Liberapay takes 0% platform cut on recurring donations (processor fees remain). Any future Bazaar schedule must be offered clearly **before** a seller accepts it. Incumbent terms change. Sources: [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/sell), [Ko-fi](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002506494-Does-Ko-fi-take-a-fee), [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com/about/faq).

## What receiving a real pilot means

The [existing Bazaar operational work](https://github.com/coesite-operations/capability-market-services-section/issues/5) governs eligibility and supplier qualification; [Shop](https://github.com/causeway-services/shop.561.group) remains its own merchant. An application is a demand or supply signal, **not** consent to use a private payment account. Participation is subject to applicable terms, appropriate licenses and Indigenous and other data sovereignty.

We will distinguish applications, verified consenting shops, current buyable offers, initiated checkouts, independently settled payments, delivered work, refunds and resulting net creator earnings. Nothing gets described as a sale or a new customer merely because it appeared in a GitHub issue.
