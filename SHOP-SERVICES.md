# Shop Services — supplier-backed merchandise and contextual shop advertising

**For an independently operated shop that wants to sell more kinds of things or reach more customers.** Bazaar's intended business is not only directing customers to shops. Shops should also be able to **buy optional services** from competent providers: merchandise production, packaging, fulfillment, professional creative work and disclosed advertising across approved publisher sites.

[Describe the actual service your shop needs](https://github.com/causeway-services/.github/issues/new?template=bazaar-shop-service.yml), or [contact Causeway privately](mailto:causeway@561.group?subject=Bazaar%20Shop%20Services%20inquiry). GitHub is public: never attach customers' addresses, payment data, unreleased artwork, private reseller terms or keys.

**Current state:** These are **qualified inquiries / a supplier-pilot prospectus**, not two live instant-checkout SKUs. We do not yet advertise an accepted automated TPOP reseller API, bookable network inventory, fixed pricing or a successful third-party merchant purchase. We will name the accountable service seller, supplier, full terms and accepted price before any fee or commitment.

## 1. Merchandise production as a service

Bring a design or content you have the rights to sell—or ask for an eligible creator/merch preparation service. The service can comprise print-file readiness, product/variant selection, manufacturer comparison, quoted production and shipping, print-quality sample review, branded fulfillment and actual post-sale recovery.

**How upstream-fulfilled sales could work:** your original Shop charges its customer its own retail price through its own accepted merchant checkout. A separately qualified POD supplier produces and ships when the specific customer order is authorized. The supply cost, shipping, taxes, FX, repairs/returns and any separately chosen Bazaar service fee are disclosed *before* the merchant commits. The shop remains responsible to its customer unless a separate actual agreement allocates obligations differently. Customer artwork and buyer addresses go to suppliers only under exact rights and privacy terms.

**Observed competitor and potential supplier: [TPOP](https://www.tpop.com/en/).** The French platform offers an all-in-one hosted shop, POD, branded parcels and documented Shopify, WooCommerce and Etsy integrations. Its [current pricing tiers](https://www.tpop.com/en/pricing) include Free, Unlimited and Business; published [seller terms](https://www.tpop.com/en/page/terms) distinguish original retailer receipts from separately debited printer/shipper charges and state customer-delivery responsibilities. [TPOP's own billing explanation](https://help.tpop.com/en/impression-a-la-demande/invoicing-and-payment-of-your-orders/how-will-tpop-charge-me) uses a *demonstration*, not a quote: €30 retail and €14 printer/shipping cost would leave €16 **before** card fees, taxes, losses, support, ads or venue fee. That is NOT the merchant's net profit.

TPOP is **not yet a contracted 561 Group upstream provider**. The public integration list is **not** a documented general-purpose TPOP reseller order API. A proposed Bazaar-managed supply account for multiple outside shops needs actual written authority and clear manufacturer/seller/buyer custody. No TPOP product image, protected catalog or third-party brand artwork is licensed for us to syndicate just because it is visible online.

We also have real original production capability to use where qualified: [Red Cup print-design engineering](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/print-design-engineering-services-section), the [Causeway Fine Print Mercantile source and production runbook](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-print-on-demand-services-section/blob/main/PRINCIPAL-RUNBOOK.md), and the already-existing [Printify provider-draft work](https://github.com/causeway-services/public-media-services-section/issues/16). Those sources are not proof of finished, purchasable merchandise. Supplier sourcing must fairly compare TPOP with suitable other providers on the **same item and destination**.

**Initial deliverable for an authorized shop:** a source- and rights-qualified product and supplier quote identifying actual blank/size/print method, live price and currency, destination shipping/VAT, print-area/artwork master, seller/payee, expected delivery, reprint/refund terms and consumer obligations. Physical sample, customer checkout and provider order submission each require their own authorized receiving. Supplier selection is not a shipment.

## 2. Advertise a shop across consenting network sites

Shops can inquire about paid **contextual sponsorship placements** on participating 561 Group properties, using the [existing Red Cup house advertising engine](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/web-advertising-system-engineering-services-section). The present engine explicitly supports `house`, `sponsored` and `affiliate` creatives with disclosure, dates and relevant page context. The [original ad site](https://github.com/red-cup-engineering/house-ads-server-site) and every publisher must independently accept and actually serve any paid placement.

The first sensible unit is an **agreed fixed-period contextual sponsorship** on a specifically authorized publisher + slot + topic. A shop supplies an original product/service URL, concise rights-cleared copy, an optional suitable image and an indicative budget. Causeway checks placement rights, relevant reader value, disclosed paid relationship, placement dates, creative acceptance and a quotation. Ads do **not** buy a privileged Bazaar search ranking, access to other shops' customers, behavioral profiles or undisclosed endorsements. A shop can use free discovery without buying an ad.

The original engine identifies house contexts for properties such as [Koios.News](https://koios.news/) and [Play](https://play.561.group/); their actual **live advertiser inventory, present viewability, publishing rights and traffic are not verified here**. We will not imply all 561 domains are automatically advertising inventory. The current sampled/first-party view events are not independently verified human impressions, so there is **no invented CPM, guaranteed impressions, conversions or billable view count**. A contractually accepted *time/placement* could be independently evidenced before per-impression selling becomes trustworthy.

**Initial deliverable for a shop:** one specific offered publisher/slot/context and duration, creative/editorial rights and disclosure, named actual ad seller, agreed price/total budget and cancellation/complaint terms, source-verified deployment/placement, actual publisher receiving and campaign closeout. Until that happens, a form submission is only an inquiry—not an accepted ad purchase or booked placement.

## Shop-controlled economics, not an obligatory fee cascade

These services are **independently chosen**. A creator may only use Bazaar discovery; may buy POD supplier management; may buy one contextual ad; may buy both under separately presented terms; or may use its original direct suppliers and advertisers. **Neither listing a Shop nor buying printing authorizes ad charges.**

For every actual order, separately identify: (1) the customer and original retail merchant, (2) upstream printer/fulfiller and its supply invoice, (3) any optional venue/management fee and payee, (4) the shop advertiser, (5) consenting publisher(s) and their contracted compensation where applicable, (6) accepted delivery, adjustments, refunds and net creator earnings. Do not call gross retail receipts or suggested margins realized seller profit.

Our existing [Bazaar seller application](https://github.com/causeway-services/.github/issues/new?template=bazaar-seller.yml) is for a **seller entering the marketplace**. The new [Shop Services inquiry](https://github.com/causeway-services/.github/issues/new?template=bazaar-shop-service.yml) is for a **Shop buying a service**. Those are different customer jobs; no public application grants an ad-spend budget or outreach permission.
